What the Protect Package covers
  • Identity verification. Document, biometric and liveness checks against passport, driving licence and Home Office digital identity standards.
  • Right-to-Work. Home Office share-code validation, IDSP-aligned digital RTW, and automated re-checks before expiry.
  • 5-year address history. Automated gap-analysis flags every unexplained period, with worker-supplied evidence requested in-app.
  • 5-year employment history. Reference requests, chase-ups and consolidated employer responses, with a full audit trail of attempts.
  • Criminal record (DBS). Basic, Standard and Enhanced DBS applied for, tracked and stored against the worker profile.
  • Credit & CCJ check. Where BS7858 financial probity applies, soft-search credit reference and CCJ identification.

How it works

From offer letter to first shift in four steps.

02

Every step is digital, time-stamped and exportable as evidence. No chasing references, no hunting for documents.

1

Invite the candidate

Trigger a branded onboarding link by email or SMS. The candidate completes identity, right-to-work and consent on any device, with no account to create.

2

Automated BS7858 vetting

The platform requests five-year history, references, criminal record and credit checks in parallel, and flags every gap for action rather than waiting in sequence.

Site induction & sign-off

Push role and site-specific modules. The worker e-signs, and your team receives a real-time pass or fail notification against that assignment.

A live status board, automatic refresher and expiry reminders, and a one-click ACS or COP119 evidence pack whenever an auditor or client asks.
