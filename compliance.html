WHAT WE MAP TO

The standards behind every check.

BS7858:2019

BS7858: Screening in secure environments

The UK standard for vetting personnel working in environments where security and safety matter.

  • Identity, RTW, 5-yr address & employment
  • Criminal records (DBS), financial probity
  • Gap analysis & written justification
  • Re-screen at 36-month intervals (configurable)
SIA ACS

SIA Approved Contractor Scheme

Pass the SIA's voluntary approval scheme assessment with confidence.

  • Pre-built ACS evidence pack
  • Assignment instructions module
  • Licence expiry tracking
  • Continuous improvement record
COP119

BSI COP119: Code of Practice

Best-practice guidance for the security guarding industry. Workflows aligned to clause-by-clause requirements.

  • Worker selection & screening
  • Training & competence records
  • Supervision & quality control
  • Complaints & non-conformance log
UK GDPR · DPA 2018

Data protection by design

Lawful processing, retention controls, DSAR support and consent-based screening.

  • Lawful basis recorded per data category
  • Configurable retention & deletion
  • DSAR export & redaction tools
ISO 27001

Information security

UK-hosted, ISO 27001-certified data centre infrastructure with role-based access control.

  • Encryption in transit & at rest
  • MFA & SSO (Enterprise)
  • Penetration tested annually
  • Detailed audit logs
