SERVICES

Everything you need to screen, induct and stay compliant.

From BS7858:2019 Protect screening to live audit packs, our team and platform cover the whole lifecycle of a compliant workforce.

BS7858 PROTECT PACKAGE

Government-standard background screening, fully automated.

Built around BS7858:2019, the UK standard for screening of individuals working in secure environments. Every check runs in parallel and is evidenced in a single tamper-proof file.

Identity verification

Document, biometric and liveness checks against passport, driving licence and Home Office digital identity standards.

Right-to-Work

Home Office share-code validation, IDSP-aligned digital RTW, and automated re-checks before expiry.

5-year address history

Automated gap-analysis flags every unexplained period. Worker-supplied evidence requested in-app.

5-year employment history

Reference requests, chase-ups and consolidated employer responses, with full audit trail of attempts.

Criminal record (DBS)

Basic, Standard and Enhanced DBS: applied for, tracked and stored against the worker profile.

Credit & CCJ check

Where BS7858 financial probity applies: soft-search credit reference and CCJ identification.
