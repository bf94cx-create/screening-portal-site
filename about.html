OUR MISSION

Replace paperwork with proof.

Every worker we screen is one less compliance risk for our customer, and one more individual who gets onboarded with dignity, clarity and speed. We exist to make safe recruitment effortless for the operators who do the hard jobs that keep the country running.

From manned-guarding contractors to domiciliary care providers to multi-vendor labour suppliers, we build the same foundation: a single, tamper-proof record per worker, ready for any inspector, any client, any time.
